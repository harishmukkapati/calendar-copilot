Paper 4 Grading Rubric (100 points), fictional

Thesis (20 pts): The thesis is clear, specific, and arguable, and appears in the opening paragraph.
Argument (30 pts): Reasons are well organized and each premise is explained and supported.
Objection and reply (20 pts): At least one strong objection is stated fairly and answered convincingly.
Use of readings (15 pts): Accurately explains and cites at least two assigned readings.
Writing and citations (15 pts): Clear prose, correct Chicago author-date citations, within the word limit.

Late policy: papers lose 5 points per day late.
